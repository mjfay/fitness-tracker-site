Privacy Policy

Fitness Tracker is a personal-use application operated by and for a single person (its owner). It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app reads the owner's own Google Health data (exercise sessions, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, weight, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation and sleep) and reads and writes the owner's own Google Sheet.

How data is used and stored

The data is used only to fill in the owner's personal training log. It is stored only in the owner's own Google Sheet. The app's OAuth tokens are kept in the owner's own secured accounts and are used for nothing else.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, transferred to, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the data at any time from the Google Sheet and can revoke the app's access at any time in their Google Account settings.
